## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Bar & widgets" icon="bars">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Launcher" icon="rocket">
    App search, calculator, emoji picker, and wallpaper browser in one keystroke. [Explore](/noctalia/launcher/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Control Center" icon="setting">
    Quick settings, shortcuts, and screen time from a single panel. [Explore](/noctalia/control-center/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Theming & palette" icon="sun">
