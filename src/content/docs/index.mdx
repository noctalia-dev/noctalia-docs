## Runs on your compositor

Niri, Hyprland, KDE, Sway, Labwc, and Mango — see [compositor setup](/noctalia/compositor-settings/niri/) for the specifics.

:::caution[Moving from v4?]
v5 installs alongside v4 and does not migrate its settings. Both can coexist on most distributions — update your compositor keybinds to launch the new commands.
:::
