---
title: Trash
---

Move files to Trash, restore them, or delete them permanently.

## Move to Trash

Press `Delete` or choose **Move to Trash** to send the selected native items to Trash. This also works on search results from different folders: only the selected items move, and the search stays active.

You can also drag native files and folders onto **Trash** in Places, including from another Luciole window. Copy-only drags, `Ctrl` copy, `Alt` action choices, remote files and archive extractions are rejected. A failed move never turns into permanent deletion.

:::note
Trash, restore and Empty Trash need the system's Trash service and a session D-Bus connection. See [troubleshooting](/luciole/troubleshooting/) if they do nothing.
:::

## Restore or delete trashed items

In Trash, **Restore** returns the selected items to their original locations and asks about conflicts. **Delete Permanently** removes them for good and follows **Settings → General → Confirm Permanent Deletion**.

## Empty Trash

**Empty Trash** is a toolbar button whenever Trash is open. It is also in the context menu of the Trash row in Places and of the Trash folder background.

It stays disabled when Trash is empty or unavailable. Hidden contents count. The confirmation shows the measured contents and size, and it always appears.

## Take items out of Trash

To move top-level items to another local folder, drag them onto a folder or a Places destination, or use `Ctrl+X` and `Ctrl+V`. The original folder may be missing.

File Operations reports the move and asks about existing names. For folders you can **Keep Both** or **Skip**; merging and replacing are unavailable. Remote destinations, other file systems, copy and link actions, and single items inside a trashed folder are not supported. These moves cannot be undone.

## Delete permanently

`Shift+Delete` removes the selected native items for good, including folder contents. Symbolic links are removed without touching their targets.

By default it asks first. The dialog opens with **Cancel** focused, so `Enter` cancels and `Escape` closes it. `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` cycle the controls, `Left` and `Right` switch between **Cancel** and **Delete**, and **Delete** activates once preparation finishes. Turn off **Settings → General → Confirm Permanent Deletion** to delete at once, including items already in Trash.

:::caution
Permanent deletion bypasses Trash and cannot be undone.
:::
